About us

A lifetime Mainer with over 30 years in the trade

Rock Shore Builders is a Maine carpentry and construction company with more than 30 years of experience in framing, carpentry and finish work. The work ranges from ground-up homes to a single custom tile shower, and it gets the same attention either way.

We are based in Hollis Center and we build for people who live here. That means right-sized plans, honest answers about what a job takes, and a finished product that holds up through a Maine winter.
